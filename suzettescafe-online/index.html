Why Order Ahead?

This online portal is built to make Suzette’s Café easier to visit on busy days.

Place your order online, choose a pickup time, and we’ll have everything ready at the counter. No waiting on the phone and no surprise line-ups when you just want your usual latte and crêpe.

Benefits for Guests

  • Skip phone calls and place your order any time.
  • Know your total before you arrive.
  • Perfect for regular morning or lunch routines.

Ordering Tips

Quick things to know before you order.

Before you order

  • Pickup only: Order online for pickup at the café; pay at the counter when you arrive.
  • Breakfast: Served until 11:00 AM. Daily specials: from 11:00 AM.
  • Combos: Sandwiches and wraps can be ordered as a combo (small soup/chili or Caesar/garden salad).
  • Catering: Minimum order $90; some items require a call. Contact us for details.
  • Delivery: Use Uber Eats or SkipTheDishes for delivery.
